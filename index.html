My Story

I'm a Computer Science student at the University of Ibadan and an active member of the ACM. I work across the full stack, but I'm happiest on the frontend — where code meets design and interfaces come alive.

On the backend and automation side, I built a tool that scrapes the Billboard Hot 100 with BeautifulSoup and syncs it straight into a Spotify playlist — no manual updating, just a fresh chart-topping playlist on autopilot.

Outside of web dev, I do motion graphics: video editing, AI upscaling, and frame interpolation in After Effects. I like that it makes me think about timing and polish the same way good UI does.

In short: a full-stack-leaning student who codes with intent, automates the boring stuff, and is always building the next thing.
